# Designs Overview

The `designs` category is for reusable visual direction documents rather than implementation rules.

## What Belongs Here

- Theme briefs for a product or feature.
- Color palettes and semantic color roles.
- Typography direction and component mood.
- Layout and visual-language references for a surface.

## What Does Not Belong Here

- Implementation constraints that should apply everywhere. Those belong in `rules`.
- Step-by-step execution workflows. Those belong in `skills`.
- Architectural rationale. That belongs in `decisions` or `concepts`.

## Suggested Metadata

- `theme`: short theme name.
- `palette`: named color family or token set.
- `surfaces`: product areas this design applies to.
- `tags`: search terms such as `dashboard`, `marketing`, or `dark`.
